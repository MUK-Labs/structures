How the estimate works

The first tap belongs to the boundary shown under “Next boundary”—usually the start of bar 1, even when the recording has leading silence. Pickups, changing meters and the diagram’s original bar labels are already accounted for. You can start later by choosing another boundary.

Each tap reads the recording’s current media time. Pausing, buffering or changing playback speed does not change the score count. Press P to play or pause; Backspace undoes a mark while the capture area has keyboard focus. If you miss a bar, choose the correct next boundary or undo and replay the passage.

Entry positions between your marks are interpolated in score beats. This is an estimate: rubato, fermatas and timing within a bar may need later refinement. Entries outside the marked span stay unavailable. Repeated score passages need one continuous pass through the diagram; do not count a repeated bar as the next new bar.

Finish with the final displayed boundary to cover the end of the piece or excerpt. You can also finish early and save a partial map. Your marks never replace the published timing until an exported file is integrated into the repository.
