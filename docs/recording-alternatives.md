# YouTube recording review

Checked on **27 November 2026**. The user reported frequent ads on Ashkenazy's Op. 87 no. 4 and requested substitution of potentially troublesome recordings throughout the database, accepting the loss of Shostakovich timing work.

## What changed

The original 59 recording records were checked against live YouTube oEmbed titles and channel names. **35 records belonged to Topic channels**: 12 Ashkenazy, 12 Jane Carlson, 6 Roberto Prosseda, and one each for Krebbers, Kocsis, Takács Quartet, Schiff and Amadeus Quartet. These 35 records have been removed from active piece files and replaced by performer-published concert or educational sources. The earlier Sacco alternative already covers Shostakovich no. 4, so the active database now contains **58 recording records across 52 pieces**.

This is a provenance-based substitution, not a measurement that every Topic upload had ads or that its replacement has monetization disabled. **No affirmative nonmonetization statement was found for the replacement videos.** YouTube can place ads even where the uploader has not monetized a video. The replacements must therefore be treated as candidates, not certified ad-free sources.

## Timing and old links

- Each replacement has a new recording ID and an empty alignment map. No timestamps were copied between performances. Shostakovich's published maps are empty, as authorized.
- A saved draft or applied timing for a retired recording cannot attach to its replacement. Those old local records are ignored; this update does not claim to erase browser storage on every user's device.
- URLs requesting a removed recording fall back to the piece's current source. The viewer and timing tool update the selected recording in the URL.
- The [manual timing checklist](manual-timing.md) and [blank worksheet](manual-timing-template.csv) list the current sources only. The previous repository worksheet contained no entered seconds, so it was regenerated without losing contributed values.
- Multi-piece videos use absolute timestamps on the full upload. Uploader chapter cues help navigation; they are not measured barline attacks or alignment anchors. Read the recording scope now displayed on the timing page before tapping Space.

## Evidence and limits

Each piece records the checked primary YouTube URL, date, performer and verification method. New sources were identified from live YouTube title/author/description metadata, with performer websites used where available. Public playback or embedding flags are metadata checks, not proof of successful playback in every browser and not monetization indicators.

The earlier short watch-page and embedded-playback checks of Sacco and Sviridov no. 4 showed no ads in that session. This observation is not generalized to the other uploads. No full-performance or repeated classroom-use ad audit is claimed.

Useful primary corroboration includes [Sofia Sacco's project](https://sofiasacco.com/shostakovich-50), [Simone Sgarbanti's video page](https://www.simonesgarbanti.com/video), the uploaders' descriptions linked above, and [WDR's Mozart performance page](https://www1.wdr.de/orchester-und-chor/sinfonieorchester/videos/video-wolfgang-amadeus-mozart---adagio-und-fuge-in-c-moll-kv---100.amp).

YouTube explains that [ads can appear without uploader monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2475463?hl=en), that [monetized videos do not always show ads](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7438625?hl=en), and that [embedded videos follow the upload's ad settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/132596?hl=en). Structures cannot guarantee ad-free YouTube playback by changing a player parameter or selecting a channel category.
