## How to evaluate a replacement

1. Verify the exact work, movement and performer from the uploader's description, preferably corroborated by the performer or producer.
2. Try the embedded player in the timing page, including several seeks and a sustained listening pass. Record any observed ads or embedding errors with the date; a single clean start is only a limited observation.
3. Keep the original recording ID and its anchors. Add a new ID and an empty map for each different upload or performance.
4. Review the new source's barline timing before using entry playback. A chapter label helps navigation but is not enough to align a diagram.

YouTube says [ads do not always appear even on monetized videos](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7438625?hl=en). It also says [rights holders or YouTube may place ads even when an uploader has not monetized the video](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2475463?hl=en). Therefore neither a small channel nor a currently clean playback is a reliable “no ads” classification. The practical approach is to keep alternative performances available and record actual experience.
