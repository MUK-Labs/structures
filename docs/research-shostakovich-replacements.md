# Shostakovich recording replacement audit

Checked on **27 November 2026**. All twelve Ashkenazy Topic uploads formerly used for Op. 87 nos. 1–12 were removed from the active database at the user's request. Their replacement is Sofia Sacco's own-channel filmed cycle. No. 4 retains the previously added Ruslan Sviridov alternative as well.

These are **performer-published alternatives, not verified nonmonetized videos**. No explicit claim that monetization is disabled was found. YouTube can serve ads on uploads that the uploader has not monetized, so channel identity alone does not establish ad-free playback. See [YouTube's explanation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2475463?hl=en).

The exact number, key, performer/channel and fugue chapter were checked from each live YouTube watch page. All twelve pages reported player status `OK` and `playableInEmbed: true` on the check date. This is metadata verification, not an end-to-end playback or advertising test. No. 4's brief browser playback observations are retained in its own recording metadata; they must not be generalized to the other eleven uploads.

The performer's [Shostakovich 50 project page](https://sofiasacco.com/shostakovich-50) and [producer's project page](https://fotw.london/sofia-sacco-shostakovich) corroborate the filmed cycle. The videos were located through [Sacco's complete-cycle playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZCQhHPxlw6p960jVgypqH0fxDDiGIAiC).
