About Structures

A visual listening atlas for counterpoint and recurring themes. Rows follow voices or, in thematic-form maps, thematic families. Colors identify related material.

Click an entry to play its passage in a recorded performance. Hover or focus an entry for a notation preview. The preview shows the theme’s first occurrence; later entries may be transposed or transformed.

Playback follows recording-specific alignment points. YouTube seeking and synchronization can have a small timing tolerance; this is not sample-accurate audio editing.
